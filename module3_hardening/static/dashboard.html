AiTM Phishing Security Training & Awareness Module Module 4 (Josna)

Essential awareness concepts designed for employees and security analysts to understand and defend against proxy phishing campaigns.

🎣 1. What is AiTM Phishing?
Traditional phishing steals passwords via fake static forms. Adversary-in-the-Middle (AiTM) inserts a transparent reverse proxy between you and the genuine login page (e.g., Microsoft or Google). It relays your login requests live, so even genuine MFA prompts work—allowing the attacker to intercept your final authenticated session cookie!
🔄 2. Why MFA Can Be Bypassed
SMS codes, email OTPs, and mobile authenticator push notifications authenticate you to whatever server your browser is visiting. Because your browser is talking to the proxy, you hand the one-time code to the attacker, who relays it immediately before it expires.
🔑 3. Why Passkeys & FIDO2 Stop It
Passkeys and FIDO2 hardware keys (YubiKeys) use Origin Binding. Your browser kernel cryptographically verifies the exact URL in the address bar. If you are on evil-proxy.com, your browser refuses to send credentials meant for login.microsoft.com. The proxy gets nothing!
⚠️ 4. Warning Signs to Look For
• Look at the address bar: Typosquatted domains (e.g. login-micros0ft.com).
• Unusually slow page loads or weird URL hopping.
• Browser security certificate warnings or unknown certificate authorities.
• Sudden re-prompts for MFA after logging in.
🚨 5. What To Do If Compromised
1. Immediately go to your genuine account portal and choose "Sign out everywhere".
2. Revoke active app sessions and OAuth tokens.
3. Notify your internal Security Operations Center (SOC).
4. Enroll a FIDO2 hardware security key or platform passkey.
🛡️ 6. Token Binding (DPoP)
Even if an attacker steals an access token via a proxy, OAuth 2.0 DPoP (RFC 9449) binds the token to your computer's private cryptographic key. When the attacker tries to replay the stolen token from their computer, the server blocks them!

B) The proxy relays the 6-digit code to the real service in real time, capturing the resulting session cookie.
